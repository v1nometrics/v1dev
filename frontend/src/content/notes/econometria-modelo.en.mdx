Before opening Jupyter, write:

1. **Dependent variable**: what you want to explain
2. **Regressors**: what you think explains it
3. **Fixed effects / controls**: what you need to "clean"
4. **Identification hypothesis**: why your estimate is causal (or not)
5. **Standard errors**: clustered? robust? bootstrap?

```latex
Y_{it} = \alpha + \beta X_{it} + \gamma Z_{it} + \mu_i + \epsilon_{it}
```

<Callout type="tip">
If you cannot write the equation, you do not know what you are estimating.
</Callout>

## Why this matters

- Forces clarity about what you are testing
- Avoids variable "fishing"
- Documents methodological decisions
- Makes code review and replication easier
