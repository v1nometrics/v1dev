---
title: "Quick heuristic: when to use Postgres + pgvector?"
date: "2026-01-16"
tags: ["ml", "infra", "postgres"]
summary: "If you want a simple stack and moderate volume, pgvector is usually the best default."
---

Use pgvector when:

- You **already use Postgres** and want to avoid "yet another database"
- Your vectors **fit reasonably in RAM/SSD** (under ~10M vectors)
- You **measure recall/latency** and accept controlled approximation
- You want **ACID transactions** with your vector data

Consider alternatives when:

- Volume is very high (over 100M vectors)
- You need native sharding
- Multi-tenancy is complex with strong isolation
- p99 latency under 10ms is critical

<Callout type="decision">
pgvector is the "boring technology" of vector search. When in doubt, start with it.
</Callout>
