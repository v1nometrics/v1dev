## Minimum SLOs for a search API

| Metric | Target | Window |
|--------|--------|--------|
| p95 latency | < 300ms | 7 days |
| 5xx error rate | < 0.1% | 7 days |
| Availability | ≥ 99.9% | 30 days |

<Callout type="warning">
If you do not measure, you do not know. Define SLOs before saying "it works".
</Callout>

## How to measure

```sql
-- p95 latency (if you log into a table)
SELECT percentile_cont(0.95) WITHIN GROUP (ORDER BY latency_ms)
FROM api_logs
WHERE endpoint = '/search'
  AND created_at > NOW() - INTERVAL '7 days';
```

## Error budget

With 99.9% availability over 30 days:
- **Budget**: 43 minutes of downtime
- **Burn rate**: monitor how much of the budget is being consumed
